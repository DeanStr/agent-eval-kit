# Parlance Labs evals and Agent Eval Kit

**Assessment:** Agent Eval Kit already implements substantial evaluation execution, evidence management, and revision comparison infrastructure. Parlance Labs provides a broader methodology for deciding what to evaluate, discovering failures, validating evaluators, and improving AI products. The strongest opportunity is to add human-led discovery and measured judge reliability to AEK's existing harness.

**Current implementation direction:** The subsequent [practical improvement exercise](marketing-improvement-exercise-2026-09-10.md) supported a smaller next step: strengthen pilot review and good/defect calibration in the existing authoring skill. The infrastructure proposals below are deferred possibilities, not the current engineering roadmap. Build them only when repeated use demonstrates a concrete need.

This comparison covers public materials available on September 10, 2026 and AEK version 0.4.0 at commit `7a97ab5d30507bc94fdd9ecd65ba5730185556bd`. Public descriptions establish what the course promises and what the public skills prescribe. They do not establish the quality of private teaching, advisory work, or student implementations. AEK capabilities below are grounded in its source, documentation, and relevant local tests; they are not measurements of live model accuracy.

**The current course goes substantially beyond writing judges.** The linked syllabus describes nine core lectures and an interview-preparation bonus. Its sequence is: build a support agent and specify permissions; instrument nested traces; create a deterministic synthetic commerce world; review traces and form a failure taxonomy; validate binary evaluators; build regression gates and production monitoring; red-team the agent; improve accuracy; and optimize cost. Concrete teaching tools include the OpenAI Agents SDK, Langfuse, ClickHouse, GitHub Actions, and promptfoo. Later work compares configurations, uses held-out evaluation, and addresses reward hacking and bounded improvement loops. This is the September 2026 curriculum; older course reviews can describe a materially different version. [5. Current syllabus](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1KSUlzN-C8z1q_gHduCi6eKgCzlHVV6u3dT0aypqi_YI/edit).

**The most important scope distinction is the role of the agent.** Their main subject is an AI application being evaluated: a support assistant, RAG pipeline, or tool-using agent. AEK uses an agent to evaluate a software project or artifact, which can itself be AI-powered but need not be. Our browser collector can test a conventional storefront; our command checks can evaluate deterministic software; our text and image paths can assess existing artifacts. [AEK evaluation types](../evaluation-types.md), [6. Product-evaluation definitions](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/).

This distinction changes the roadmap. AEK needs better target traces if it wants deep AI-agent diagnosis. It does not need to turn every existing browser, command, or artifact workflow into an AI observability system.

**Their method starts with observed failures and expert judgment.** The public guidance prioritizes looking at actual outputs, translating observations into specific failure modes, and using code checks wherever possible. Subjective judgments should have clear decision boundaries. Their judge-writing skill calls for one failure mode per evaluator, labeled examples, and structured critiques. Their broader guide shows how expert feedback improves the judge itself. [7. Judge-writing skill](https://github.com/ai-evals-course/evals-skills/blob/main/skills/write-judge-prompt/SKILL.md), [8. LLM judge guide](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/llm-judge/index.html).

There is empirical motivation for keeping that expert involved. Shreya and coauthors' EvalGen paper describes how evaluation criteria evolve while people inspect model outputs. An interview can establish explicit requirements, but examples can reveal preferences and failure definitions that the interview missed. [9. Who Validates the Validators?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12272).

Their July 2026 experiment examined 100 apartment-leasing traces with 39 human-labeled failures. General coding agents were competitive with dedicated platforms, and all tested systems found additional issues. They also missed failures requiring unstated product context. The authors recommend an interactive human review loop and note that the systems generally failed to interview them. This is one small, non-public dataset, not a ranking benchmark or evidence that any tool catches all important failures. [10. Do Automated Evals Work?](https://parlance-labs.com/blog/posts/auto-evals/index.html).

For AEK, this supports both the subscription-agent approach and the product-owner interview. It also exposes the remaining gap: a good interview should lead into reviewing examples, revising expectations, and preserving the resulting labels.

**AEK has several substantial strengths worth preserving.** These are demonstrated capabilities of our implementation; comparing them with educational material does not establish superiority over systems built by Parlance or its students.

First, AEK keeps an observed product failure, an abstention, and an execution error distinct. `cannot_judge` contributes no earned or judgeable weight; unavailable evidence reduces coverage. Critical gates separately prevent a high weighted score from masking a required failure. This is especially valuable when an autonomous loop needs a reliable stop or rejection signal. [Scoring](../../src/scoring.ts), [score arithmetic](../../src/score-slice.ts), [CI decisions](../../src/ci.ts).

Second, the harness validates the evidence contract. Requested rubric and case identities must match; citations must be in scope; screenshots require actual access; blocked scenarios cannot support fabricated defects. These controls make model judgments inspectable and reject malformed evaluations. They still cannot prove that a model interpreted valid evidence correctly. [Shared contracts](../judging-contracts.md), [identity validation](../../src/judging/contracts.ts), [rubric evidence validation](../../src/judging/rubric.ts).

Third, collection and judging are separable. Sealed observations can support a new judging experiment without rerunning a browser workflow, and receipts distinguish changes to target and evaluation method. This is an excellent base for future calibration because it can hold the evidence fixed while the judge changes. [Evidence workflows](../evidence-workflows.md), [collection sealing](../../src/collection.ts), [receipts](../../src/receipts.ts).

Fourth, comparison already accounts for more than a score delta. Campaigns retain completion and coverage information, withhold incompatible measurements, and provide repeated-run or paired-case summaries. AEK also has explicit promotion policies. These features are directly useful for evaluating revisions, provided the criteria and judges are valid. [Campaign aggregation](../../src/campaign.ts), [campaign comparison](../../src/campaign-compare.ts), [promotion](../../src/promotion.ts).

Fifth, the authoring map is more than generated YAML. It tracks discovered requirements, unresolved decisions, linked checks, actual exercise, and verified good/defect trials. It explicitly distinguishes project scope from evidence coverage. A report can therefore have complete evidence for its configured checks while the authoring map still exposes missing product requirements. [Authoring](../authoring.md), [status implementation](../../src/authoring/status.ts).

Sixth, AEK's artifact paths are concrete: text datasets, literal quotation checks, anonymous image/text comparisons, controlled captures, and file provenance. These are useful for agent-built software and creative artifacts, beyond the typical customer-support-agent curriculum. A relative preference still does not prove absolute quality, and a screenshot does not establish continuous video behavior. [Text evaluation](../text-evaluation.md), [visual evaluation](../visual-evaluation.md), [file evidence](../file-evidence.md).

**The largest methodological gap is judge validation.** AEK currently uses “calibrated” for a specific, valuable result: the same evaluation method passed a known-good target and rejected a different target with a deliberate defect, with full evidence for the check. The implementation validates those identities and outcomes. Its documentation already states that this does not estimate judge accuracy. [Authoring calibration contract](../authoring.md), [trial verification](../../src/authoring/status.ts).

Parlance's validation skill adds human-labeled examples, distinct train/dev/test roles, class-specific performance, disagreement inspection, and optional corrected estimates of production success. Their convention treats Pass as the positive class: TPR measures acceptance of human passes, and TNR measures rejection of human failures. [11. Validation skill](https://github.com/ai-evals-course/evals-skills/blob/main/skills/validate-evaluator/SKILL.md).

A concrete AEK failure remains possible: an evaluator catches an obvious injected defect but falsely passes a subtle incorrect result in most other cases. Five repeated votes can agree on that same wrong result. The evidence can be immutable and the campaign interval narrow while the measurement is systematically wrong. Our current machinery measures provenance and consistency much better than it measures human alignment.

A future calibration report should retain expert labels independently of automatic verdicts, include both decisive and abstaining judge outcomes, and bind the result to criterion, prompt, model, and evidence identities. Missing classes or too few observations should make a metric unavailable with a reason. Confidence intervals should describe the uncertainty of the actual measurement, and accepted thresholds should reflect the consequences of false passes and false failures.

Their public guidance is not a universal statistical specification. The March essay recommends precision/recall, while the validation skill emphasizes TPR/TNR for prevalence correction. These answer different questions. AEK should preserve the full confusion matrix and report explicitly named conditional rates. A production success estimate additionally needs defensible sampling and stable judge behavior; the existing weighted quality score should not silently become a prevalence estimate. [14. The Revenge of the Data Scientist](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/revenge/), [11. Validation skill](https://github.com/ai-evals-course/evals-skills/blob/main/skills/validate-evaluator/SKILL.md).

**Human review needs to support discovery and disagreement.** Current `aek adjudicate` resolves eligible automatic abstentions. It intentionally cannot override decisive automatic results or execution errors. `aek finalize` handles declared manual criteria and hybrid shares. Those boundaries make sense for changing a run's effective verdict, but do not supply an independent labeling workflow covering everything the judge said. [Adjudication](../adjudication.md), [adjudication implementation](../../src/adjudication.ts).

The missing experience is simple to describe: inspect evidence, write what is wrong, accept or reject an agent's suggested interpretation, and save an independent label. Notes should link to evidence and evolve into candidate failure definitions. Earlier samples should remain reviewable when the definition changes. Their review-loop skill makes this process explicit, including visually separating suggestions from human-confirmed observations. [12. Review loop](https://github.com/ai-evals-course/evals-skills/blob/main/skills/error-discovery/review-loop.md).

AEK should keep these annotations separate from report mutations. A reviewer who says “the judge passed this incorrectly” has produced calibration evidence, even if the original run must remain unchanged. A review interface over sealed AEK bundles would reuse existing evidence and identity controls while adding the missing human data.

**Our score design deserves more discipline, not blanket removal.** AEK maps pass to full credit, partial to configured credit, fail/not-found to zero, and abstention to unavailable. It also supports weighted automatic/manual components and diagnostic cases. Consequently, the headline score is a utility aggregation under a chosen rubric. It is not the probability that an arbitrary production request succeeds. [Scoring](../../src/scoring.ts), [score arithmetic](../../src/score-slice.ts).

Their binary preference is a useful default for atomic correctness checks. A vague “partially good” criterion is hard to label consistently. However, AEK's derived partial outcome can also represent mixed observed cases, and pairwise preference serves a different question from pass/fail correctness. These are legitimate distinctions. [7. Judge-writing skill](https://github.com/ai-evals-course/evals-skills/blob/main/skills/write-judge-prompt/SKILL.md), [case aggregation](../../src/judging/contracts.ts).

The practical change is to author more atomic criteria, reserve subjective partial credit for explicit product decisions, and display the decision-driving failures and coverage beside the score. Critical gates should continue to override aggregate success. If the metric is used for hill climbing, the evaluator should stay fixed during the comparison, with separate evaluation data used to check whether gains generalize.

**Target traces are a separate missing capability.** AEK records its own collector's tool calls and provider events. Those describe how the test was performed. They do not automatically reveal the target application's retrieval, model calls, permissions, hidden retries, or database state. A browser test can show that an AI support agent gave the wrong answer without showing why.

A first implementation could accept a strict project-owned trace export through the existing command-collector boundary. It should preserve input, messages, tool arguments/results, retrieved material, target configuration identity, and timestamps where supplied. Unsupported or malformed records should fail with actionable errors. Missing trace fields should remain explicit limitations. A full observability backend is unnecessary to prove this workflow. [Command collector](../../src/collectors/command.ts), [evidence workflows](../evidence-workflows.md).

Production monitoring adds scheduling, sampling, fresh data, and drift review beyond import. Similarly, raw evaluation-provider usage is different from the target agent's cost. AEK should keep collector cost, judge cost, and target execution measurements distinct before attempting any quality/cost comparison. [Provider usage contract](../../src/types.ts), [execution planning](../../src/execution-plan.ts).

**Synthetic generation can build on existing matrices.** Their useful idea is to define failure-relevant dimensions, review plausible combinations, and separately generate realistic inputs. AEK already executes cases and retains strata and splits; it needs an authoring workflow that produces reviewed datasets with provenance. Generated labels should not silently become expert ground truth. [13. Synthetic-data skill](https://github.com/ai-evals-course/evals-skills/blob/main/skills/generate-synthetic-data/SKILL.md), [text datasets](../../src/text/datasets.ts), [case/split tests](../../test/cases-splits.test.ts).

**Longer-term possibilities, deferred after the practical exercise:** the following sequence records the original research proposals. It does not supersede the current decision to improve authoring guidance and use the existing harness.

| Priority | Deliverable | What completion should demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Independent human labels and a minimal review interface over existing bundles | A reviewer can label automatic passes, failures, and abstentions without rewriting the original result; every label links to evidence and a criterion version. |
| 2 | Judge-validation report and disjoint calibration datasets | A fixed judge is measured against held-out expert labels, with confusion counts, class-specific rates, abstention coverage, and uncertainty; invalid data fails explicitly. |
| 3 | Error-discovery extension to authoring | Human notes become reviewed failure definitions linked to new or revised checks; existing decisions are revisited when examples expose missing expectations. |
| 4 | One strict target-trace import path | A real AI application can be evaluated and diagnosed using its internal trace alongside AEK's collection evidence. |
| 5 | Reviewed synthetic-data generation | Failure-relevant variations produce realistic cases that preserve source and split identities. |
| 6 | A published end-to-end case study | The same independently labeled task shows an actual product improvement, held-out behavior, coverage, judge limitations, and repeatability. |

These are proposed additions, not existing commands or a commitment to implement every row. The first two can start with a narrow text task: use the existing text datasets and fixed evidence, add independent labels, and compare a judge with those labels. That would test the core design before involving browser-state variability or production telemetry.

For the case study, use both obvious and subtle defects. Include a known false-pass temptation, a case that requires product context, and insufficient evidence. Establish the expert labels before revealing judge results where practical. Compare the original and improved target with the same evaluator, then show separately whether improving the evaluator changes the conclusions. This would supply stronger evidence than a passing harness test suite alone.

**Positioning should make AEK's job explicit.** A defensible product description is: “AEK turns agreed product requirements and collected evidence into repeatable evaluations for coding-agent improvement loops.” The strongest concrete promises are provider choice, project-owned criteria, inspectable evidence, separate uncertainty, and revision comparison. Statistical judge reliability should become an evidence-backed claim only after the proposed validation workflow exists. [Project overview](../../README.md).

The public skills are complementary enough to test alongside AEK without rebuilding the harness. Their discovery process can produce failure definitions and labels; AEK can collect, judge, compare, and gate revisions. The integration boundary should be explicit artifacts rather than an assumption that installing both automatically connects them. Their public repository supplies agent instructions and generated workflow guidance, not evidence of a maintained runtime equivalent to AEK. [3. Public repository](https://github.com/ai-evals-course/evals-skills).

The course may be worthwhile for direct expert feedback, guided practice, and team alignment. AEK's existence does not replace those benefits. The accessible material identifies useful methods without requiring private course access. The immediate engineering step is the authoring refinement described above; a dedicated human-label or judge-validation workflow should depend on demonstrated need.

**Evidence limits and verification.** Eight relevant local test files passed during this comparison: scoring, shared judge contracts, authoring, cases/splits, campaign aggregation, campaign comparison, CI thresholds, and adjudication. The command used was `node --import tsx --test --test-reporter=dot` with those files. No live model calls, target production monitoring, private course access, or independent reproduction of Parlance's experiment were performed. No runtime implementation was changed.

The repository's existing release records include broader test and behavioral-trial history, with their own limitations. Those records support software contract confidence, not generalized judge accuracy or proof that author interviews capture every user's intent. [Release validation](../release-validation.md).
