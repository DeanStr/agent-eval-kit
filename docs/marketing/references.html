A higher bar for the climb.

Three references informed the redesign: a landscape that makes search tangible, clear evidence attached to outcomes, and visible branches that explain how a better result was found.

01 / The strongest illustration reference

Google DeepMind · AlphaEvolve

The wireframe landscape turns an abstract optimization process into a physical place. Light, depth, and elevation give the image a clear focal point.

Applied to AEK: an original procedural terrain, a luminous route, and evaluated checkpoints. Height is conceptual; scores stay on evidence cards.

Our direction

A landscape with evidence behind each move.

The revised AEK artwork is generated from original vector geometry. Its route and revision labels are interactive, with keyboard selection, playback, and reduced-motion support. An unverified candidate appears outside the terrain so missing evidence never becomes an invented position on a score axis.

The demo distinguishes responsibilities: your coding agent or orchestration makes and selects changes; AEK evaluates and compares them. The illustrated results are synthetic, and the mountain represents a search space rather than a measured performance surface.
